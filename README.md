
# Mahalakshmi Milk Procurement Analysis

## Project Description
This project focuses on analyzing the milk procurement and sales data of **Mahalakshmi Milk Factory** over a period of nine months. The goal was to identify inefficiencies in milk collection, sales, and profit generation, and to propose actionable solutions to improve business operations.

Through this project, I conducted data analysis, identified key problems, and suggested strategies to increase efficiency and profitability. I also interacted with professionals and company owners to validate findings and recommendations.

---

## Key Problems Identified
- Low milk collection compared to factory machine capacity.
- Lower sales and profits of milk and milk products.
- Inefficient utilization of milk collection resources.
- Limited usage of collected milk in alternative profitable ways.

---

## Solutions & Insights
- Proposed methods to increase milk collection from suppliers.
- Strategies to improve sales and profitability of milk products.
- Recommendations for better utilization of milk collectors and machines.
- Identified new ways to leverage collected milk to reduce waste and increase revenue.

- **Final Report:** Complete analysis with actionable insights and recommendations.
